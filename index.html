Denizalp Aybak (b. 1997) is an Istanbul-based fashion photographer and filmmaker.

Having graduated first in his class in Management at Sabanci University, Aybak approaches image-making with both strategic precision and artistic intuition.

His work is built around atmosphere, with every creative decision made to evoke feeling.
