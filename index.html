denizalp aybak (b. 1997) is a photographer and filmmaker, based in istanbul and available internationally.
having graduated top of his class
in management at sabanci university, he brings a strategic approach to image-making, considering both the work itself and the world it belongs to.
his work creates atmosphere through contrast: light that feels dark, beauty that appears distant, stillness that carries tension, and precision that emerges from spontaneity.
editorial collaborators include: vogue türkiye, harper’s bazaar türkiye,
elle türkiye, l’officiel türkiye
brand collaborators include: vakko, bee goddess, zeynep arcay,
peugeot, skechers, lc waikiki
